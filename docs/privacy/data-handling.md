# Your data in Qlicker

Your institution uses Qlicker for teaching, class participation, and assessment. This page explains what is saved and who can see it.

## What is collected?

Qlicker saves your account details, including your name, email, role, and student number if supplied. It also saves your preferences, courses and activities you join, answers, submission times, grades, feedback, chat messages, votes, and uploads. Quiz answers are saved as you work, before you select Submit. Sign-in information, IP addresses, and service logs help run and support the service. Cookies and browser storage keep you signed in and remember preferences.

## Who can see it?

Your instructors and assigned teaching assistants can see named coursework, participation, grades, and feedback, and can access IP addresses recorded with named answers. Other participants can see chat and answers or statistics shared with the class, but not your private grades. The administrator and authorized support staff can access information needed to manage and support the service.

## What does anonymous mean?

Anonymous activities hide your identity from instructors. Answers from the same person may appear together under a respondent label. The administrator can identify the account behind a response. Avoid including identifying details in your answers. Anonymous activities have no grades.

## Are other services involved?

Your institution may use external services for sign-in, email, hosting, storage, video, or AI. If AI is enabled, conversations and generated results are saved, and relevant course content may be sent to the AI service. This can include answers an instructor asks AI to summarize or help grade. Ask the administrator which services are used.

## How long is information kept?

Your institution determines how long records, logs, and backups are kept. Archiving a course or chat does not necessarily delete its information. Ask the administrator about retention and deletion at your institution.

## Questions or corrections?

Contact the administrator or your institution’s privacy office to ask about your information, request access or corrections, or learn about your institution’s privacy policy. For questions about grades or participation alternatives, contact your instructor.
